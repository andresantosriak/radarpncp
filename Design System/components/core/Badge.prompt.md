Status pill — opportunity status, modality, urgency, "Novo". Soft by default.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>Forte aderência</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning">Analisar</Badge>
<Badge tone="danger">Descartado</Badge>
<Badge tone="gold" solid>Urgente</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">Dispensa eletrônica</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral · brand · gold · success · warning · danger · info`. `dot` adds a leading status dot, `solid` fills it.
